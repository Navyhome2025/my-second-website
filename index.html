Bloxburg

Bloxburg is a life simulator game on Roblox where you can get a job, roleplay, and build your house.

    Jobs

  • Pizza Delivery
  • Pizza Baker
  • a cook or cashier at Blox Burgers
  • A cashier or stocker at Bloxburg Fresh food
  • ice cream seller at Ben's Ice Cream
  • Hair dresser
  • Pizza Delivery
  • Mechanic
  • Fisherman
  • Miner
  • Janitor

    • Because there are so many Jobs it is more likely you'll find a job you enjoy. Bloxburgs Job Mechanic makes it so that you can gain levels in your Job. This increses your Pay and when you hit level mile stones you can recieve rewards making any job worth investing your time. There is no bad job, However, if you want the most possible payout, I would reccomend being a cashier at Blox burger because it is very fast paced.
